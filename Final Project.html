Conclusion :

In this project, I explored the electric vehicle population dataset to investigate the relationships between electric range, vehicle make, and model year. I used descriptive visualizations such as a histogram, boxplot, and scatterplot to understand how electric range is distributed and how it differs between manufacturers and over time. The histogram showed a highly skewed distribution with most vehicles having low range. The boxplot and summary statistics highlighted clear differences between makes, with Chevrolet and Tesla having higher average ranges. The scatterplot indicated a positive relationship between newer model years and higher electric ranges.

For inferential statistics, I conducted an ANOVA test to determine whether electric range significantly differs between makes. The results showed a statistically significant difference, suggesting manufacturer choice is a major factor in EV performance. I also ran a correlation test to evaluate the relationship between model year and electric range, which showed a strong statistical relationship, further confirming the improvement in EV technology over time. Overall, the analysis provides insight into how electric vehicle capabilities have advanced and vary significantly by manufacturer.
